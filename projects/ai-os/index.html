RIIC Office AI Operating System

The office now has one AI system, on its own laptop, that does the work and never acts without the team.

ChallengeThe problem

The office had no single system for its work. Email, meetings, memos, minutes, graphics and follow-up were all happening by hand, across different tools, and that data could not leave the office.

ActionsWhat I built

I built the office AI operating system on Nod, with a local harness that runs on the office's own laptop, and turned the separate workflows into a single operation. It has its own skills, capabilities and tools, a main brain, and a backup brain: if the first model fails, the work moves to the backup. Because it runs locally, the office controls its data. Nothing goes to external parties, and the data is not used to train models or exposed.

It never sends or does anything without the team's approval. That human check is what makes it a solid agentic loop: the system can draft, log, remind and prepare the work, but the team gives permission before anything goes out.

It has its own main folder on Google Drive, and that folder holds the context that grounds the AI. An activity log with tabs for project management and work management records every task, who did it, and whether it is completed, pending or rejected. It logs urgent emails, drafts replies and sends those drafts to the team, then updates the log when the team takes action. It runs the office email: it classifies and labels every incoming message into 8 categories, messages me on anything urgent, and keeps the inbox organised.

It sends a morning briefing and an end-of-day briefing, and logs both. Meeting minutes go into a minutes folder. It has calendar access: it saves events, knows the office members' emails, adds them as invitees, and can create, edit or delete events, including Google Meet links. Memos are saved and tracked in a memo folder. It can make graphics in the school colours in Canva, and it reminds the team of work already done and what is still pending.

ResultsWhere it stands

The system is running in the RIIC office as one operation, on the office laptop. Email, briefings, minutes, memos, calendar and follow-up now sit in one place, with a log of who did what.

If the main model fails, the backup takes over. Nothing goes out unless the team approves it, and the office keeps control of its data.
