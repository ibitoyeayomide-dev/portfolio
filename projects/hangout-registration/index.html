Event Registration and Ticketing System

Paid registration for a social event, from receipt upload to a personal message on the day.

  1. Challenge
  2. Actions
  3. Results

ChallengeThe problem

The hangout needed paid registration, proof of payment, and a way to keep the list and the messages in order. Doing that by hand would have meant chasing receipts, typing names into a sheet, and writing to people one by one.

ActionsWhat I built

I built a Lovable landing page and a registration form (email, full name, WhatsApp number, receipt upload). An n8n webhook received each submission, saved the receipt to Google Drive, logged a pending record in Airtable, and sent a registration-pending email.

Separate published workflows handled ticket approval and errors, including its own error workflow. On the day, each registered person got a personalised message with their name, the venue and the 2:00 PM time.

ResultsWhere it stands

The registration system went live and was used for the hangout. Over 45 people registered.

Receipts landed in Drive, registrations landed in Airtable with verification status and ticket price, and people received a personalised day-of message.
