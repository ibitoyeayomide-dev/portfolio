Questions people ask

What do you build?

AI agents, automated workflows and AI operating systems for teams that lose hours to repetitive work, like sorting an inbox, writing minutes, sending certificates or handling event sign-ups.

Which tools do you work with?

n8n, Google Apps Script, Airtable and Google Workspace for the workflows. OpenRouter, DeepSeek and Ollama for the AI. Docker, Cloudflare Tunnel and Vercel to deploy and host it.

Will the AI act without my team?

Not on anything that matters. I build so the AI does the sorting and drafting, and a person approves anything that goes out.
